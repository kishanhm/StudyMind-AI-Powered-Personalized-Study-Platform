The Algorithm

How behavior prediction works

01
Circadian profiling
Your wake time and chronotype are mapped to a circadian rhythm model. We calculate your cortisol peak window (alertness), post-lunch dip, and second-wind window with biological precision.
02
Ultradian rhythm modeling
The brain naturally cycles through 90–120 minute rest-activity rhythms. StudyMind aligns your session blocks with these cycles to maximise retention and minimise fatigue.
03
A* optimal scheduling
Tasks are assigned to time slots using the A* search algorithm. Each state represents a partial schedule; g(n) accumulates mismatch penalties, and h(n) lower-bounds the remaining cost. The globally optimal assignment is found efficiently via a min-heap priority queue.
04
Cognitive load matching
The A* cost function penalises hard tasks in low-energy slots and wastes of peak windows on easy tasks. Critical tasks carry a time-preference penalty so they are placed as early as possible in the day.
05
Adaptive rescheduling
As you check off sessions and log completion quality, the model updates your energy curve. A* is re-run on every change so your schedule is always globally optimal, not just locally greedy.
